The Leaders

The men guiding the container. Each brings a different lineage of study and lived experience to the work, united in service to remembrance, responsibility, and legacy.

Co Founder

Mason Mostajo

Guardian of Sacred Masculinity

With a life devoted to truth, love, and humanity, Mason Mostajo serves as a guardian of sacred masculinity and a keeper of the ceremonial spine. Through deep curiosity, cultivated compassion, and inner alchemy, he moves in sacred service to life and the human spirit. As co founder of Brotherhood of the Roots, Mason leads initiatory spaces for men and co ed communities, shaping culture with a generational lens grounded in remembrance, responsibility, and legacy.

Guided by the Rainbow Warrior prophecy and carrying Eagle medicine, he serves as a living bridge between ancestral earth based wisdom and future generations.

Shaped by a long standing career in high level sport and years living with chronic pain, Mason initiated a path of self discovery that transmuted injury into insight and suffering into service through mastery of emotional and relational intelligence, paired with ancestral connection to spirit. Through breath, brotherhood, and belonging, Mason catalyzes life force liberation and empowers sovereign embodiment in service to the Most High.

Co Founder

Alec Vishal Rouben

Entrepreneur & Connector

With over a decade as a purpose driven entrepreneur and more than 15 years as both student and teacher of yoga, Alec Vishal Rouben stands at the intersection of business, mindfulness, and brotherhood. Rooted in a lifelong study of grounded spirituality, heart coherence, and conscious leadership, he brings the same depth of presence and curiosity to his ventures as he does to the mat of life. Alec's entrepreneurial journey has been guided by service, building communities, businesses, and experiences that uplift others while aligning with integrity and truth.

As co founder of Brotherhood of the Roots, Alec invites men to reconnect with purpose, emotional strength, and authentic connection, bridging ancient practices with modern understanding. His work is fueled by a devotion to human nature, tending spaces where men will remember their power, lead themselves with compassion, and live in alignment with their True Self.

A teacher of life and student of spirit, Alec embodies the path he teaches, walking with humility, vision, and an unwavering commitment to the collective awakening of brotherhood and humanity. Alec's mantra is LOVE LIFE, and you will be able to feel that in the spectrum he brings through in any circle you are a part of.

Facilitator

Cody ThunderEagle Watts

Clinical Therapist & Group Work Facilitator

Cody ThunderEagle Watts is a professional people helper serving as a clinical therapist, university professor, life coach, and group work facilitator. He believes mastery is cultivated through committed practice, a willingness to engage discomfort, and an ever growing capacity for joie de vivre. With over a decade of experience, Cody brings a grounded, integral approach to leadership and personal development.

Dancing at the intersection of the profound and the absurd, he creates spaces that are both supportive and challenging. Through a contagious playfulness and hard won wisdom, he invites people to embrace paradox, meet themselves fully, and live in ways that are meaningful, grounded, and in connection with something larger than themselves.
