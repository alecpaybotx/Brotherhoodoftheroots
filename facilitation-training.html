Facilitation Training

World leading experts in the Men's Work industry come together with BOTR leaders and men devoted to learning the tools, processes, and trauma-informed understanding on how to safely facilitate this work in a sacred and powerful way.

What you will learn

This training is for the man who feels called to hold space — for his family, his community, or professionally. We cover the full arc of facilitation: from nervous system regulation and trauma-informed practice to circle structure, ritual design, and crisis response.

  • Trauma-informed facilitation principles
  • Holding sacred space — structure, ritual, and container
  • Somatic and embodiment facilitation tools
  • Men's Work lineage and ethical framework
  • Crisis response and care protocols
  • Supervised practice and mentorship

Who this is for

Men who are ready to lead. Men with lived experience in this work who feel the pull to serve others walking the same path. Men who take the responsibility of holding space seriously.

Next cohort

Training cohorts are kept intentionally small to ensure depth of mentorship and quality of learning. Join our community or reach out directly to be notified when the next cohort opens.
