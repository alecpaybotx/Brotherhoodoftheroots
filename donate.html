Support the Work

Your gift keeps this container open for the men and women who need it most.

Every gift you give helps us continue this deep work. Brotherhood of the Roots exists because people show up, hold space, and give what they can so others can do the same. Your donation is a direct investment in that circle staying open.

Where your donation goes

Donations go directly into scholarship support for men who could not otherwise afford to attend. They help cover the cost of our monthly council meetings and fund scholarship spots for our multi day events and trainings, so that no one is turned away because of what is in his wallet.

Give directly through Venmo. Every dollar goes back into this work.

Give on Venmo

Prefer another way to give, or have a question about where funds go? Email us at brotherhoodoftheroots@gmail.com or message us on Instagram @brotherhoodoftheroots.
