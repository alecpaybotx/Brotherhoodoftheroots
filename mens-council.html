Men's Council

A sanctuary of ritual and consistency where "iron sharpens iron." We hold one another accountable to align our words with our actions, repotentizing our word as our bond through radical ownership.

What to expect

Men's Council is a regular gathering space built on truth-telling, accountability, and deep brotherhood. We sit in circle, we speak honestly, and we hold each other to the standard we've each committed to.

  • Regular council gatherings in a structured, held container
  • Somatic and embodiment practices
  • Accountability structures and brotherhood bonds
  • Mentorship from experienced facilitators

Who this is for

Men who are ready to stop performing and start showing up. Men who want to be known — truly known — by other men. Men who are done going it alone.
