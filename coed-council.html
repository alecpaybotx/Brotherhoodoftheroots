Way of We

Two Pathways in the Way of We

There are two ways to walk this container with us. Join our recurring gathering in Boulder, Colorado at Vali Soul Sanctuary, where sacred masculine and sacred feminine meet in community. Or step fully into village consciousness at our multi-day Way of We event, held the first weekend of March at the same sanctuary grounds.

Both pathways are described below, starting with the multi-day event.

Way of We Multi-Day Event

The first weekend of March, we gather for three full days of village consciousness at Vali Soul Sanctuary in Boulder, Colorado. Friday opens the container, Saturday moves deep into the work, and Sunday closes it in reflection and integration. Lunch is provided each day.

  • Friday4pm to 8pm
  • Saturday9am to 5pm
  • Sunday9am to 3pm
  • LocationVali Soul Sanctuary
  • Seats40 total, 50/50 men and women
  • FacilitationUncle Vincent Matoska, with a woman elder to be announced

The Way of We

We are rooted in community together, so let us do the work together. Join us as we do sacred masculine and sacred feminine work in the Way of We, our monthly co ed container.

We can only truly rise as a society if we join together in these trying times. We are here to support the conversations that need to happen between the sacred masculine and the sacred feminine so we can all heal and rise together.

We are here to remember our calling and the calling of all the generations before us who may never have had this opportunity to heal our relationships. Remember, we have the choice to be whole and to be free.

  • Monthly co ed gathering circles
  • Relational healing practices
  • Communication and intimacy workshops
  • Community celebrations and seasonal gatherings

Why it matters

We are not meant to heal in silos. The masculine and feminine need one another to remember wholeness. This council is that reminder.
