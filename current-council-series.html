Beneath the Surface

Monthly Council Series  ·  October 2026 to January 2027

Four councils. Four dimensions of the inner life. One brotherhood holding the container.

The Four Councils

One per month  ·  October through January

October 15

ROOT

Wednesday  ·  6–9 PM  ·  Boulder Starhouse

"What grounds us?"

We begin at the origin. The mother wound, the father wound, the places where the root system of a man was interrupted. Core wound alchemy. Coming home to what anchored you before the world reshaped you.

November 12

RELATION

Thursday  ·  6–9 PM  ·  Boulder Starhouse

"Who do we protect and provide for?"

Provider and protector. Our relationship to the feminine, to children, to innocence. How we show up for those we love and what it costs us when that instinct goes unexamined.

December 10

REST

Thursday  ·  6–9 PM  ·  Boulder Starhouse

"What needs to deepen to regenerate?"

The council of verbal gold. What is given freely in this circle becomes the gift of the season. Rest is not collapse. It is the intelligent withdrawal that makes the next season possible.

January 6

RENEWAL

Wednesday  ·  6–9 PM  ·  Boulder Starhouse

"What is ready to emerge?"

We close the series where every real journey ends: at the threshold of what is next. Not the next project, not the next goal. The next version of the man you are becoming.
